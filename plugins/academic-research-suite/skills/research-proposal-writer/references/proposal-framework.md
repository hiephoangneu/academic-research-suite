# Research Proposal Framework

## Default architecture

1. Title
2. Executive summary
3. Problem and significance
4. State of knowledge and unresolved gap
5. Research questions
6. General and specific objectives
7. Conceptual or theoretical framework
8. Hypotheses or propositions
9. Research design and methods
10. Ethics and data management
11. Work packages and timetable
12. Expected scholarly outputs
13. Practical, policy, or societal impact
14. Risk register and mitigation
15. Budget and justification
16. Team capability and governance
17. References

Override this structure when an official template exists.

## Alignment matrix

| Element | Must align with |
| --- | --- |
| Problem | Gap, beneficiaries, and significance |
| Question | Objective, theory, data, and method |
| Hypothesis | Construct definitions, mechanism, and estimator |
| Work package | Objective, deliverable, owner, timing, and budget |
| Output | Evaluation criteria, dissemination route, and acceptance measure |
| Impact | Supported pathway, stakeholder, and adoption condition |

## Objective test

Each specific objective should:

- use one clear action
- produce observable evidence
- remain achievable within resources and duration
- connect to at least one question and work package
- avoid embedding an assumed result

## Method feasibility test

Confirm:

- realistic access to population, data, organisations, or archives
- sample-size logic
- instrument availability and validation
- appropriate software and expertise
- sequencing of qualitative and quantitative components
- time for approvals, piloting, collection, cleaning, analysis, and dissemination
- fallback when access or response rates fail

## Output quality

Specify quantity, quality standard, completion evidence, responsible person, delivery date, and dependency for every output.

## Risk register fields

Risk, probability, impact, early warning, prevention, contingency, owner, and residual risk.

## Verification note

List every current regulation, form, rate, deadline, eligibility condition, and institutional rule that was verified, including source and access date.
