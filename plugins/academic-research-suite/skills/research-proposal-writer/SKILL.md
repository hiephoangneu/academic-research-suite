# Research Proposal Writer

## Resolve the proposal context

1. Identify the funding or assessment scheme, jurisdiction, institution, discipline, project duration, budget ceiling, required template, language, and evaluation criteria.
2. Read all supplied forms and instructions before drafting.
3. Verify current legal, institutional, funding, formatting, and eligibility requirements through official sources.
4. Treat repository examples and older forms as structural aids, not current regulatory authority.

## Build the proposal logic

1. Define the practical problem and the theoretical problem separately.
2. Synthesize the closest literature into unresolved tensions, not a chronological catalogue.
3. State a research gap that produces a consequential question and a feasible design.
4. Align the general objective, specific objectives, questions, hypotheses or propositions, data, methods, work packages, and outputs.
5. Make expected contributions theoretically meaningful and empirically testable.
6. Explain why the context, sample, period, and level of analysis are theoretically relevant.

## Design the methodology

- Match qualitative, quantitative, mixed-method, experimental, quasi-experimental, survey, archival, panel, PLS-SEM, fsQCA, or case-study designs to the research questions.
- Define population, sampling frame, unit of analysis, sample-size logic, instruments, constructs or variables, data sources, analysis sequence, validity safeguards, ethics, and data management.
- Address endogeneity, common-method bias, measurement validity, missing data, robustness, and generalisability when relevant.
- Do not promise methods, access, sample sizes, outputs, or causal identification that the project cannot credibly deliver.

## Plan delivery

1. Convert objectives into work packages, milestones, responsibilities, deliverables, and measurable acceptance criteria.
2. Link each budget item to a work package and permitted cost category.
3. State implementation, dissemination, policy, managerial, educational, or societal pathways without inflating impact.
4. Identify operational, data, ethical, methodological, scheduling, and dissemination risks with mitigation actions.

## Integrity rules

- Never invent regulatory requirements, citations, partners, permissions, datasets, preliminary results, costs, or institutional commitments.
- Clearly label illustrative budgets, schedules, models, and unverified sources.
- Avoid presenting a fashionable method as a contribution.
- Avoid generic novelty claims based only on country, sector, or technology.
- Avoid em dashes and unsupported superlatives.

## Deliver

Follow the supplied form exactly when one exists. Otherwise, use the proposal architecture in the reference file and provide a clear assumptions and verification note.
