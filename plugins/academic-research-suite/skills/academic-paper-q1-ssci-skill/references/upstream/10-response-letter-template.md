## Response Principles

1. **Address EVERY comment.** Even minor comments require a response.
2. **Be respectful and grateful.** "Thank you for this valuable
   observation" is a strong opener.
3. **Be specific.** Reference exact sections, pages, and line numbers.
4. **Quote revised text.** Show the reviewer exactly what changed.
5. **Justify disagreements with evidence.** "We respectfully maintain
   our original approach because [cite evidence]."
6. **Never be defensive.** Frame disagreements as scholarly dialogue.
7. **No em dashes** in the response letter.
