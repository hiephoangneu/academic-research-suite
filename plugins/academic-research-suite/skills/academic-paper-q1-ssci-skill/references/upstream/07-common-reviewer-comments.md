# Common Reviewer Comments and How to Address Them

> Based on patterns from Q1 SSCI journal review processes across
> education, management, psychology, and economics.

---

## Category 1: Introduction and Research Gap

### Comment 1.1
> "The research gap is not clearly articulated."

**How to fix:**
- Add a dedicated 1-2 paragraph gap statement before the purpose statement
- Use explicit language: "Despite..., no study has...", "However, the
  relationship between X and Y remains underexplored in [context]."
- Explain WHY the gap matters (theoretical and practical consequences)

### Comment 1.2
> "The introduction lacks a compelling rationale for the study."

**How to fix:**
- Open with a striking statistic or real-world problem
- Strengthen the "So what?" factor: explain consequences of the gap
- Add significance statement covering theoretical, practical, and
  methodological contributions

### Comment 1.3
> "The research questions are vague or too broad."

**How to fix:**
- Each RQ should specify: variable(s), population, and context
- ❌ "What is the effect of technology on learning?"
- ✅ "To what extent does the use of adaptive learning platforms predict
  academic achievement among first-year university students in Vietnam?"

## Category 2: Literature Review

### Comment 2.1
> "The literature review is more of a summary than a critical analysis."

**How to fix:**
- Reorganize by themes, not by individual studies
- Add comparative language: "While Smith (2023) found..., Jones (2024)
  reported contrasting results..."
- Include critical evaluation of methodological quality

### Comment 2.2
> "Key references are missing."

**How to fix:**
- Search multiple databases (Web of Science, Scopus, PsycINFO)
- Include seminal works AND recent publications (last 3 years)
- Check reference lists of key articles for overlooked sources
- Ask colleagues in the field to review your reference list

### Comment 2.3
> "The theoretical framework is underdeveloped."

**How to fix:**
- Dedicate a full subsection (500-800 words) to the theory
- Explain core constructs and their relationships
- Justify why THIS framework (not another) is appropriate
- Show how the framework connects to each RQ/hypothesis

## Category 3: Methodology

### Comment 3.1
> "The sample size is too small / not justified."

**How to fix:**
- Add a priori power analysis (G*Power, reference: Faul et al., 2009)
- For qualitative: discuss data saturation with evidence
- If small, acknowledge as limitation and report post-hoc power

### Comment 3.2
> "The validity and reliability of instruments are not adequately reported."

**How to fix:**
- Report Cronbach's alpha for each scale (in your sample, not just original)
- Conduct and report CFA if using established scales in a new context
- Describe content validity procedures (expert panel review)
- Report factor loadings if applicable

### Comment 3.3
> "The data analysis methods are not appropriate for the research questions."

**How to fix:**
- Create a clear mapping: RQ1 → Analysis Method → Justification
- Explain assumptions tested and met
- If assumptions are violated, explain the chosen robust alternative

### Comment 3.4
> "Ethical approval is not mentioned."

**How to fix:**
- Add: "This study was approved by [Institution] IRB (Protocol No. [X],
  approved [date])."
- Describe informed consent and data anonymization procedures

## Category 4: Results

### Comment 4.1
> "The results section contains interpretation."

**How to fix:**
- Remove all evaluative language from Results
- ❌ "Interestingly, the results showed..."
- ✅ "The results showed..."
- Move all "why" and "what it means" content to Discussion

### Comment 4.2
> "Statistical reporting is incomplete."

**How to fix:**
- Report: test statistic, degrees of freedom, exact p-value, effect size
- Example: *F*(2, 187) = 14.32, *p* < .001, partial eta-squared = .13
- Include 95% confidence intervals where possible
- Report non-significant results with the same detail

### Comment 4.3
> "Tables/figures are unclear or redundant."

**How to fix:**
- Ensure each table/figure adds unique information
- Add descriptive titles and notes
- Reference each table/figure in the text before it appears
- Remove any data presented in both table and text form

## Category 5: Discussion

### Comment 5.1
> "The discussion merely repeats the results."

**How to fix:**
- For each finding, use this structure:
  1. Brief restatement (1 sentence)
  2. Comparison with prior research (2-3 sentences)
  3. Theoretical interpretation (1-2 sentences)
  4. Practical implication (1-2 sentences)

### Comment 5.2
> "The limitations are too generic."

**How to fix:**
- ❌ "This study has several limitations."
- ✅ "The cross-sectional design precludes causal inferences. Future
  research should employ longitudinal designs to examine temporal
  relationships between X and Y."
- Pair each limitation with a specific future research suggestion

### Comment 5.3
> "The implications are not actionable."

**How to fix:**
- ❌ "These findings have implications for practice."
- ✅ "Based on the finding that formative feedback significantly predicted
  self-regulation, educators should incorporate structured feedback cycles
  at least twice per instructional unit."

## Category 6: Writing Quality

### Comment 6.1
> "The writing is unclear / needs professional editing."

**How to fix:**
- Shorten sentences (target 15-25 words)
- Use active voice
- Remove hedging where evidence is strong
- Eliminate redundant expressions
- Consider professional editing services

### Comment 6.2
> "The manuscript is too long / needs to be more concise."

**How to fix:**
- Check journal word limit
- Remove redundant information across sections
- Tighten literature review (synthesize more aggressively)
- Move supplementary analyses to appendices

---

## Category 7: References

### Comment 7.1
> "References are outdated."

**How to fix:**
- Aim for 40%+ from the last 5 years
- Replace older references with newer ones where possible
- Keep seminal/foundational works regardless of age
- Add "recent" section to literature search

### Comment 7.2
> "Self-citations appear excessive."

**How to fix:**
- Limit self-citations to under 10%
- Only self-cite when genuinely relevant
- Replace with independent sources where possible
