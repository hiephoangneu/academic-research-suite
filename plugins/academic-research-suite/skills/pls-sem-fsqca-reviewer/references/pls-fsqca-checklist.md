# PLS-SEM, fsQCA, and NCA Checklist

## PLS-SEM measurement

### Reflective constructs

- indicator reliability with content-validity judgement
- internal consistency using appropriate coefficients
- convergent validity
- discriminant validity, preferably HTMT with inference
- cross-loadings only as supporting evidence

### Formative constructs

- content validity
- convergent validity through redundancy analysis when feasible
- indicator collinearity
- weights, significance, and relevance of loadings
- no automatic deletion of nonsignificant indicators

### Higher-order constructs

- specify reflective or formative nature of both levels
- justify repeated-indicator, embedded two-stage, or disjoint two-stage approach
- report lower-order assessment before higher-order conclusions

## Structural model

- structural collinearity
- path coefficients and bootstrapped confidence intervals
- explanatory power and effect sizes
- predictive relevance where justified
- out-of-sample prediction with benchmark comparison
- mediation using specific indirect effects
- moderation using interaction effect, simple slopes, and plotted uncertainty
- endogeneity assessment when theory and design indicate risk
- robustness to plausible model alternatives

## MICOM and MGA

1. Assess configural invariance.
2. Assess compositional invariance.
3. Assess equality of means and variances when full invariance is claimed.
4. Run MGA only after the necessary invariance condition.
5. Justify group construction theoretically and avoid arbitrary median splits.

## fsQCA

- disclose calibration rule and anchors for every condition and outcome
- inspect membership distributions and exact crossover cases
- report necessity consistency and coverage
- state frequency, consistency, and PRI thresholds
- disclose directional expectations and logical-remainder treatment
- report intermediate and parsimonious solutions when core conditions are interpreted
- report raw coverage, unique coverage, and solution consistency
- test reasonable alternative calibration anchors and thresholds
- analyse absence of outcome separately when asymmetry is claimed

## NCA

- justify ceiling-line technique
- report effect size and permutation test
- report bottleneck table at substantively meaningful outcome levels
- discuss practical relevance and data range

## Integration questions

1. Does each method answer a distinct question?
2. Are construct definitions and calibration conceptually aligned?
3. Do net effects and configurations converge, complement, or contradict?
4. Does necessity evidence agree across NCA and fsQCA?
5. Does the theory explain asymmetry and equifinality?
6. Are managerial implications derived from supported configurations rather than isolated conditions?
