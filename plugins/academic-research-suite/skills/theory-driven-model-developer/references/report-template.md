# Complete Literature Review and Research Model Report Template

Use this as the default structure for a user requesting a complete report. Adapt emphasis to the project and study design, but do not omit crucial traceability without indicating a reason. Write substantive academic narrative, not only tables.

## A. Research concept and coverage statement

- Proposed title and central research question
- Focal phenomenon, outcome, theoretical target, population and unit of analysis
- Input type: idea, topic, or supplied model; assumptions and preserved baseline
- Review approach; actual searched platforms, latest search date, source access level and limitations
- Executive summary of candidate contribution and evidence confidence

## B. Empirical literature review

- Thematic synthesis of the foundational and recent empirical landscape
- Cluster evidence by focal relationship, mechanism and boundary, not author chronology
- Contradictory/null evidence, evidence quality and transferability
- Explicit **empirical evidence matrix** with actual studies, designs, contexts, findings, access levels and citations

## C. Theoretical foundations and critical synthesis

- Verified foundational origins and evolution of selected theoretical perspectives
- Core assumptions, explanatory mechanisms, theoretical predictions and limits
- Comparison of competing lenses and rationale for integration/non-integration
- Theoretical mechanisms to be developed, revised or tested
- **Theory-to-construct/relationship map** with cited foundational works

## D. Research gaps and contribution

- Established consensus and verified points of disagreement
- Ranked gaps, supporting and counterevidence, why gaps matter
- Principal precise gap and one to two subordinate gaps if warranted
- Testable research question(s)
- Anticipated theoretical contributions stated as **proposed**, not demonstrated

## E. Research hypotheses and analytical logic

- Narrative hypothesis-development subsection per relationship with evidence, counterevidence and mechanism
- H1, H2a, H2b, etc. in a coherent causal/temporal sequence
- Conditional, nonlinear, mediation, moderation or configuration claims only where justified
- **Hypothesis traceability table:** ID, antecedent, consequent, form, mechanism, theory, verified supporting and counter evidence, design caveat

## F. Constructs and measurement conceptualisation

- **Full construct dictionary:** label, acronym, precise definition, role, dimensions, level, timing, overlaps/differences, verified source provenance
- Candidate measures and validated scale citations where verified; otherwise mark pending
- Reflective/formative/composite and higher-order choices as provisional until justified
- Candidate control and rival constructs, with cautions against post-treatment controls

## G. Proposed research model

- Figure or legible directed-arrow specification covering all H#; show mediator and moderator structure explicitly
- Model legend defining names, arrows, proposed signs/forms, levels and timing
- Compare lean baseline, preferred model and at least one credible rival
- Table of differences from user's original model if one was supplied
- Research design and feasible testing priorities; distinguish observed associations from causality

## H. Final audit, open questions, and references

- Evidence confidence and unresolved verification matters
- Critical limitations of web-only coverage, source access, theory choice and identification
- Suggested additions to literature search or data design
- Verified APA 7 reference list only; unresolved works in a separate verification queue
- Actual search log and source ledger, if not included in the main report

## Suggested prose quality

Write in a precise, critical voice suited to journal-ready Economics/Finance/Business scholarship. Establish the debate, evaluate evidence, derive a theoretical tension, then develop hypotheses. Avoid one-paragraph-per-author inventories, generic claims such as `few studies exist`, and inflated novelty language. Include relevant citations directly with each substantive external claim.

## Ten-point quality gate (all must pass or be marked incomplete)

1. Every bibliographic reference exists and its supplied metadata has been verified.
2. Every material empirical assertion matches what the accessed source actually supports.
3. Search provenance, source access and coverage boundaries are visible.
4. Contradictory evidence and influential rival mechanisms are fairly represented.
5. Foundational theories are correctly attributed and used within their original or justified extended scope.
6. Each research gap is specific, consequential, source-grounded and addressable by the study design.
7. Every hypothesis has a transparent mechanism and a falsifiable prediction, with evidence calibration.
8. All constructs are conceptually separable, level-aligned and operationalisable without fictitious scale provenance.
9. Each preferred-model arrow matches its hypothesis, and baseline/rival models have been compared.
10. The report distinguishes proposed contributions from verified discoveries, association from causation, and an integrative web review from systematic coverage.

## Input-to-output tests

- **Idea:** `How do generative AI capabilities shape SME innovation?` -> develop alternative scope formulations as needed, evidence-based theory, complete report and final compact model.
- **Topic:** `Digital financial inclusion and entrepreneurial resilience in emerging economies` -> compare mechanisms and contexts, nominate constructs and testable hypotheses, justify boundaries.
- **Preselected model:** `Privacy concern -> readiness -> adoption, moderated by trust` -> preserve the supplied model, examine construct levels and reverse directions, accept/revise/reject paths with reasons and produce a complete report.

The examples demonstrate workflow and make **no** claim that the proposed constructs or paths have already been validated in existing studies.
