# Manuscript Quality Gates

## Contribution test

Accept a claimed contribution only when it answers all five questions:

1. What established explanation, assumption, or boundary is being changed?
2. Through what mechanism is it changed?
3. Under what conditions should the change hold?
4. What evidence can discriminate the new account from plausible alternatives?
5. Why does the change matter beyond the study context?

Context, sample, method, or technology alone does not constitute theoretical contribution.

## Section gates

| Section | Required pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Accurate phenomenon, relationship, and context; no causal overclaim |
| Abstract | Purpose, data, method, principal result, and contribution are traceable |
| Introduction | Specific debate and gap lead directly to the research response |
| Literature review | Competing arguments are synthesised and unresolved tension is explicit |
| Hypotheses | Theory, mechanism, direction, level, and boundary are coherent |
| Methods | Design choices, assumptions, measures, diagnostics, and ethics are reproducible |
| Results | Tables, text, hypotheses, uncertainty, effect sizes, and robustness agree |
| Discussion | Findings change theoretical understanding and address alternatives |
| Implications | Recommendations are supported and appropriately bounded |
| Limitations | Consequential constraints are acknowledged without ritual language |
| Conclusion | Theoretical resolution is clear and no new claim is introduced |

## Consistency map

Check every focal construct or variable across:

- definition
- theoretical role
- hypothesis
- operationalisation
- model equation
- table label
- reported result
- discussion interpretation
- implication

Flag synonym drift, reversed coding, altered reference categories, inconsistent sample sizes, and unsupported causal verbs.

## Style gate

- Prefer argument-led paragraphs.
- Put one main claim in each paragraph.
- Use concrete verbs and precise theoretical nouns.
- Remove filler, repetition, false novelty, promotional language, and unexplained abbreviations.
- Use strategic hedging where evidence is limited.
- Avoid em dashes.

## Final integrity gate

Confirm that no citation, quotation, statistic, test, page number, journal rule, or external fact was invented. Mark unresolved items visibly.
