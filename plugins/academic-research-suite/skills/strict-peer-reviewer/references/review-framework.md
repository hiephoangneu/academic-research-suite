# Review Framework

## Severity definitions

| Level | Meaning | Typical consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fatal flaw | The central claim cannot be sustained with the available design or evidence | Reject |
| Major concern | Validity, contribution, or interpretation requires substantial but feasible repair | Major revision or revise and resubmit |
| Minor suggestion | Local clarity, reporting, or presentation issue | Minor revision |

Do not inflate a fixable omission into a fatal flaw. Do not downgrade an identification failure into a reporting issue.

## Validity domains

1. **Theoretical validity:** construct clarity, mechanism, boundary conditions, competing explanations, novelty.
2. **Construct validity:** alignment between concept and operationalisation, reliability, convergent and discriminant validity.
3. **Internal validity:** confounding, selection, simultaneity, timing, reverse causality, treatment integrity.
4. **Statistical conclusion validity:** estimator assumptions, uncertainty, power, multiplicity, diagnostics, robustness.
5. **External validity:** population, setting, period, institutional context, transportability.
6. **Interpretive validity:** causal language, magnitude, null findings, asymmetry, alternative explanations.
7. **Reporting integrity:** traceability, transparency, reproducibility, ethics, complete disclosure.

## Decision matrix

| Recommendation | Minimum interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Accept | No material threat remains; contribution and execution clearly meet the journal |
| Minor Revision | Core contribution and validity are secure; required changes are local |
| Major Revision | Promising contribution, but substantial theory, design, analysis, or reporting repair is required |
| Revise and Resubmit | A potentially publishable paper requires reconstruction and a new evaluation round |
| Reject | Fatal validity problem, fundamental journal mismatch, or contribution too weak to repair within review |

The final recommendation must follow the most consequential unresolved issue, not the average quality score.

## Method routing

- Panel, causal, archival, or econometric design: invoke the panel econometrics audit when detailed estimator review is required.
- PLS-SEM, fsQCA, NCA, or integrated configurational design: invoke the dedicated method review.
- Suspicious or inconsistent references: invoke citation integrity audit.
- Requested manuscript repair: route to manuscript writing after the independent review is complete.

## Major-concern template

1. **Issue:** State the specific defect.
2. **Why it matters:** Link it to validity, contribution, or interpretation.
3. **Evidence:** Locate it in the manuscript.
4. **Required remedy:** Specify the minimum credible repair.
5. **Decision implication:** State whether failure to repair changes the recommendation.
