# Citation Verification Protocol

## Audit ledger fields

Use one row per reference:

1. Audit ID
2. Cited form
3. In-text locations
4. Reference-list location
5. Existence status
6. Author match
7. Year match
8. Title match
9. Source match
10. Volume, issue, pages, or article number match
11. DOI or URL status
12. Publication-status note
13. Claim-support status
14. Corrected entry
15. Evidence source
16. Confidence

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer:

1. publisher metadata and full text
2. DOI registry metadata
3. official government or institutional publication
4. recognised indexing database
5. author-accepted manuscript or institutional repository

Use search engines, scholarly profiles, and aggregators for discovery, not final verification when stronger evidence exists.

## Fabrication threshold

Classify a reference as likely fabricated only when:

- no authoritative or credible bibliographic trace is found after variant searches
- the journal, volume, year, pages, DOI, and authors cannot be reconciled
- the DOI resolves to an unrelated work or is structurally implausible
- the alleged article conflicts with the journal's actual record

Otherwise use “unverifiable” or “material mismatch.”

## Claim-support test

Check:

- whether the source studied the cited phenomenon
- whether the design supports the asserted relationship
- whether the sample and context support the generalisation
- whether the cited result is direct or inferred
- whether a review is cited as primary empirical evidence
- whether the source contradicts or qualifies the claim

## APA 7 repair checks

- author spelling and order
- group-author treatment
- publication year and advance-online status
- sentence case for article and book titles
- journal title and volume formatting
- issue and page or article-number accuracy
- DOI in `https://doi.org/...` form when available
- no database URL for ordinary scholarly articles
- consistent treatment of reports, laws, datasets, working papers, and webpages

Formatting repair never substitutes for existence and support verification.
